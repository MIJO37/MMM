Size

Even numbers from 20 to 200. Resizing keeps everything centered.

Points

Click the map with the Point tool to place a point, or click it again to remove it. You can also type exact coordinates.

Spawns and control points are drawn at their real in-game size. Defaults match Re:Arena (base 45, point 65); Battlefield maps use 65 and 95. The dashed ring is where players spawn.

With mirroring on, every team spawn gets a linked partner on the other side. Moving or removing one updates the other.

    Siege saucers

    Choose a saucer, then click the map with the Saucer tool. Drag a saucer to move it, right-click it to remove it, or type exact coordinates. Saucers are saved in the downloaded map file.

      Drift belts

      Choose the Belt tool, then drag across the map from where rocks enter to where they leave. Drag a belt's ends to reshape it, drag its middle to move it, or right-click it to remove it. New belts are mirrored to match the Symmetry setting, and mirrored belts stay linked.

      Moving rocks
      None

        Rock sizes on the canvas are approximate. Speed is in game units per tick (60 ticks a second).

        Boost pads

        Streamliner's Fast-Forward as a fixed pad: any ship inside gets +50% acceleration and a speed bump every tick. Choose the Boost tool and click to place a pad. Drag a pad to move it, or right-click it to remove it. New pads are mirrored to match the Symmetry setting, and mirrored pads stay linked.
